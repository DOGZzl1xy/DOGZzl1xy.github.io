# About Me

<img src="https://caihanlin.com/caihanlin.jpg" class="floatpic">

<br>Here is **Xuanyu Zhou**.

<br>I am an undergraduate student in the Urban and Rural Planning at the Zhejiang University. My research interest includes Urban Equity, Urban Mobility, LLM in Urban Planning, and Urban AI. You can find my CV [here](https://xuanyuzhou.org/file/Xuanyu_Zhou_CV.pdf). <br>

My research begins when I participated in the Qizhenwenxue Undergraduate Research Program of Zhejiang University in my sophomore year, collaborating with [Prof. Shuang Ma](https://person.zju.edu.cn/mashuang) and [Prof. Shuangjin Li](https://home.hiroshima-u.ac.jp/taofeng/team-members/shuangjin-li/). My first program is about urban resilence when cities recovery from COVID-19. We use Geographic Weighted Random Forest(GWRF) to explore the relationship between urban recovery and built environment. During this study, I contribute to debugging R code and write the Introduction and Literature Review sections. The final paper for this work can be found [here](https://doi.org/10.1177/27541231251314132). My second research program is about the connection between residents' health and built environment, togather with usage of share-bike, since residents will get both advantages and disadvantages influenced by built environment during their riding time. I mainly contribute to the GWRF and partial dependency analysis and responseble for Discussion and Conclusion section. The work has been submiited to Transportation Research Part D. My third work here is to explore how built environment influence residents' mental health. I contrubute to the whole analysis process and write the Result, Discussion, and Conclusion setion. This project is still ongoing. <br>

After my initial exploration in urban analysis, I join the Urban AI Lab founded by [Mingze Chen](https://mingzechen.com/) in my junior year. During these period of time, I conduct a detailed research into how Urban Green Space (UGS) influence demographic in shrinking cities. Using visitation data and demographic data, I complete a time-laggered research into the shrinking period of Pittsburgh, PA to explore how urban green space actually help with the revival of the city. I'm resibonsible for writing the whole draft. This work is presented in the 2024 Association of Collegiate Schools of Planning (ACSP) Annual meeting in Seattle and has been submitted to Urban Forest & Urban Green. <br>

During my senior year, I participate in the Berkeley Golbal Access (BGA) program in the College of Environmental Design in UC Berkeley. 

<br>If you are interested in any aspect of me, I am always open to discussions and collaborations. Feel free to reach out to me at — **xuanyu.zhou [at] zju.edu.cn** or **xuanyu.zhou [at] berkeley.edu**
