## Along the Way

My research begins when I participated in the Qizhenwenxue Undergraduate Research Program of Zhejiang University in my sophomore year, collaborating with [Prof. Shuang Ma](https://person.zju.edu.cn/mashuang) and [Prof. Shuangjin Li](https://home.hiroshima-u.ac.jp/taofeng/team-members/shuangjin-li/). My first program is about urban resilence when cities recovery from COVID-19. We use Geographic Weighted Random Forest (GWRF) to explore the relationship between urban recovery and built environment. During this study, I contribute to debugging R code and write the Introduction and Literature Review sections. The final paper for this work can be found [here](https://doi.org/10.1177/27541231251314132). My second research program is about the connection between residents' health and built environment, togather with usage of share-bike, since residents will get both advantages and disadvantages influenced by built environment during their riding time. I mainly contribute to the GWRF and partial dependency analysis and responseble for Discussion and Conclusion section. The work has been submiited to Transportation Research Part D. My third work here is to explore how built environment influence residents' mental health. I contrubute to the whole analysis process and write the Result, Discussion, and Conclusion setion. This project is still ongoing. 

After my initial exploration in urban analysis, I join the Urban AI Lab founded by [Mingze Chen](https://mingzechen.com/) in my junior year. During these period of time, I conduct a detailed research into how Urban Green Space (UGS) influence demographic in shrinking cities. Using visitation data and demographic data, I complete a time-laggered research into the shrinking period of Pittsburgh, PA to explore how urban green space actually help with the revival of the city. I'm resibonsible for writing the whole draft. This work is presented in the 2024 Association of Collegiate Schools of Planning (ACSP) Annual meeting in Seattle and has been submitted to Urban Forestry & Urban Greening. 

At my senior year, I chose to come to UC Berkeley as an exchange student in the College of Environmental Design. I took some class there, including Introduction to City Planning (CYPLAN110), Social and Psychological Factors in Open Space Design (LDARCH140), Transit (CYPLAN98), and Structure and Interpretation of Computer Programs (CS61a). Out of study time, I participate in the [Prof. Lu Liang's](https://ced.berkeley.edu/people/lu-liang) [Monitoring-Mapping-Modeling (3M) lab](https://sites.google.com/site/liang3mlab/home) at UC Berkeley, where I colliborate with Ph.D. student [Yuye Zhou](https://ced.berkeley.edu/people/yuye-julia-zhou). Here, I participate in an exploration about how different is between LiDAR-based Sky View Factor (SVF) and traditional street view image based SVF. This work is presented by my colliborator in Human and Geographic Dimensions of Extreme Heat and Heat Risk will take place at NSF NCAR, Colorado. 

During my last summer session, I join the MIT-UF-NEU joint summer research camp and conducting a study on the optimization of Urban Air Mobility (UAM) demand and fleet size calculation. This work is conducted with Ph.D. candidate [Xuan Jiang](https://xuan-1998.github.io/), and supervised by [Prof. Jinhua Zhao](https://mobility.mit.edu/people/jinhua-zhao/). In this study, we try to construct a new UAM demand allocation system to take dynamic ground-air demand into consideration. Meanwhile, we further introduce heterogeneous fleet into fleet size calculation and make improvement to existing GPU-based traffic simulation model, the LPSim, to departure UAM vehicles. This framework has been vaildated in a case study in the San Francisco Bay Area. The work has been submitted to Transportation Research Board (TRB) and IEEE Transactions on Intelligent Transportation Systems. 

Walking along my way, I have explored many aspects of urban planning, from urban built environment, urban equity and urban green space, street-level urban modeling and analysis, and new transportation system inside metropolitans.
