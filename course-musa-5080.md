    <h3>Verify the Claim: Philadelphia Poverty</h3>
    <p>
      Testing a news report on rising poverty with ACS tract estimates for 2006&ndash;2010 and 2013&ndash;2017. Only 36 of 376 tracts rose significantly and 27 fell; the other 83% showed no detectable change.
